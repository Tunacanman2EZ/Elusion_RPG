# Elusion RPG - Asset Contributor Agreement

Version 2, 8 October 2026

If you'd like to contribute art, audio or other assets to **Elusion RPG**,
please read these terms first. Nothing here is binding until we have both
confirmed it in writing.

**Who's who.** "Elusion Studios" is Robert Ashley Clear, the project owner,
who makes Elusion RPG. "You" are the person contributing the assets.

## 1. What you give

By sending assets under this agreement, you give Elusion Studios the right to
use, modify and include them in Elusion RPG, in anything made to promote it
(the website, screenshots, trailers, store pages), and in other Elusion Studios
projects, commercial or not, with no fees or royalties beyond any payment
agreed under section 5.

Choose one:

- [ ] **Exclusive.** These assets are made only for Elusion RPG. You won't
  reuse, resell or redistribute them anywhere else.
- [ ] **Non-exclusive.** Elusion RPG may use them, and you may also use or
  sell them in other projects.

## 2. What you promise

- You made the assets yourself, or you own the rights to them and are free to
  give the rights above.
- They aren't copied, traced or edited from someone else's work, and they
  don't come from an asset pack, a stock site or another game.
- If you used AI tools to make any of them, you'll say which assets and how.

If an asset turns out not to have been yours to give, Elusion Studios may take
it out of the game.

## 3. Where your work will be seen

The game's source code is public on GitHub, and its art and audio sit in the
same repository so the game can be built. Being public there does not make
your work free for anyone else to use: [assetlicense.md](../assetlicense.md)
lists it under your name and says it may not be reused without permission. By
agreeing, you're okay with your assets being in that public repository.

## 4. Credit

You can be credited by your name, by a handle, or not at all. Tell me which.
Credit appears in the game, in Options > Credits. If you like, share your
Discord, itch.io or portfolio link to go with it.

Whatever you choose, [assetlicense.md](../assetlicense.md) in the repository
lists your assets as yours, so nobody browsing the code takes them as free to
use.

## 5. Payment

Any payment is agreed in writing before the work starts: usually $1 to $100 per
asset or package, depending on how complex it is. Once paid, payment is final.
Future commissions, bonuses or collaborations can be talked about as the
project grows.

## 6. If you're under 18

A parent or guardian needs to read this agreement and sign it with you.
