# The first time a mage walks up to the shopkeeper. He asks; the player
# picks an answer (1, 2, 3 or 4 - a click, a tap or the number key).
# Each line is "speaker: words". hero is whoever is playing.
# "? words = branch" is a choice; "[branch]" starts what it leads to;
# "= @shop" opens the shop instead, "= @end" just ends.
shopkeeper: Are you lost?
? Lost? I was dead. = dead
? Just looking around. = looking
? Do you sell food? = food
? Trade = @shop

[dead]
hero: Lost? I remember the end. My end. And now... this town.
shopkeeper: The portal. You're not the first it has spat out.
shopkeeper: I make a living buying and selling gear. Welcome to Elusion!
? Trade = @shop
? Goodbye = @end

[looking]
hero: Just looking around.
shopkeeper: Looking is free. Buying is not.
? Trade = @shop
? Goodbye = @end

[food]
hero: Do you sell food? I'm starving.
shopkeeper: Try the fire pit. Fish don't catch themselves.
? Trade = @shop
? Goodbye = @end
